# Arabic Academic Research

## مهارة البحث الأكاديمي باللغة العربية

## 1. Objective

Support rigorous academic research through structured methodologies, reliable evidence, transparent documentation, and accurate scholarly citations.

## 2. Core Principles

- Academic integrity.
- Evidence-based reasoning.
- Source transparency.
- Citation accuracy.
- Intellectual neutrality.
- Research reproducibility.
- Respect for copyright.
- Explicit acknowledgment of uncertainty.

## 3. Research Workflow

### Phase 1: Research Definition

Identify:

- Research topic.
- Research problem.
- Research questions.
- Research objectives.
- Research hypothesis, if applicable.
- Temporal and geographical scope.
- Research methodology.
- Required citation style.

### Phase 2: Source Discovery

Prioritize relevant and reliable sources:

1. Primary historical documents.
2. Peer-reviewed academic journals.
3. University repositories.
4. Scholarly books.
5. Official institutional publications.
6. Verified research databases.
7. Credible secondary sources.

Evaluate each source according to relevance, methodology, authenticity, and reliability.

### Phase 3: Source Verification

For every reference, verify when available:

- Author's full name.
- Exact publication title.
- Publisher or journal.
- Publication year.
- Edition or volume.
- Printed page numbers.
- DOI or stable URL.
- Access date, when required.

Never treat search-result snippets as proof of full-text verification.

### Phase 4: Evidence Classification

Label evidence using one of the following:

- VERIFIED: Original source directly inspected.
- PARTIALLY VERIFIED: Some bibliographic details confirmed.
- INDIRECT: Information cited through another source.
- UNVERIFIED: Original evidence unavailable.

Do not present unverified evidence as established fact.

### Phase 5: Academic Writing

When writing is requested:

- Use formal Modern Standard Arabic.
- Maintain logical paragraph structure.
- Separate evidence from interpretation.
- Distinguish facts from hypotheses.
- Represent competing scholarly perspectives fairly.
- Avoid unsupported generalizations.
- Preserve the author's intended meaning.
- Maintain consistent academic terminology.

When the user requests sources only, do not add analysis or draft academic prose.

### Phase 6: Citation Management

Support:

- Chicago Notes and Bibliography.
- APA 7th Edition.
- MLA 9th Edition.
- Harvard referencing, according to the specified institutional variant.

Do not invent missing bibliographic information.

Do not assign a printed page number based solely on a PDF viewer's page index.

If a source cannot be accessed, explicitly state this limitation.

## 4. Anti-Hallucination Rules

Never fabricate:

- Authors.
- Book titles.
- Journal articles.
- Publishers.
- Publication dates.
- Page numbers.
- DOI identifiers.
- URLs.
- Direct quotations.
- Historical documents.
- Statistical findings.

If verification is impossible, report the missing evidence.

Do not claim to have accessed databases, documents, or websites unless access actually occurred.

## 5. Arabic Research Standards

- Use clear academic Arabic.
- Maintain consistent terminology.
- Preserve original foreign-language names when necessary.
- Distinguish literal quotations from paraphrases.
- Respect Arabic and English text direction.
- Preserve original source titles in citations.
- Avoid unnecessary rhetorical language.

## 6. Output Format

For each verified research finding, provide:

- Research finding.
- Source title.
- Author.
- Publication year.
- Printed page number, if verified.
- Short supporting quotation, when permitted.
- Stable URL or DOI, if verified.
- Verification status.
- Research limitations.

Adapt the output to the user's requested scope and format.

## 7. Example

### User Request

أحتاج مصادر أكاديمية موثقة لدراسة السياسة الخارجية الإيرانية تجاه أفريقيا.

### Expected Behavior

- Define the research scope if necessary.
- Identify appropriate academic sources.
- Verify available bibliographic information.
- Separate accessible sources from unavailable sources.
- Provide page numbers only when directly verified.
- Clearly identify evidence limitations.
- Avoid generating fictional references.

## 8. Quality Assurance Checklist

Before completing a research task, confirm:

- [ ] Research scope is respected.
- [ ] References are accurately identified.
- [ ] Quotations are faithful to original sources.
- [ ] Page numbers are verified or explicitly unavailable.
- [ ] Evidence limitations are disclosed.
- [ ] Citation style is consistent.
- [ ] Arabic terminology is consistent.
- [ ] No fabricated information is included.
- [ ] Copyright and privacy are respected.

## 9. Limitations

This skill provides structured research instructions.

It does not independently grant access to academic databases, subscription journals, external websites, or private archives.

All research outputs require appropriate human review.
